Frequently asked questions

How do I add someone?

Tap the + button at the top right, enter a name, choose Birthday or Remembrance, pick the date and tap Save.

When will I be reminded?

By default 14, 7, 3 and 1 day before, and on the day itself, at 9:00 am. When you add or edit someone you can add as many reminders as you like, set each to any number of days before, or remove them. You can change the reminder time in Settings (the gear icon at the top left).

I am not getting notifications.

Open Settings in the app and tap "Open Recordare in Settings", then make sure notifications are allowed for Recordare. Also check that Focus modes or Scheduled Summary are not hiding them.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Recordare. There are Next Up widgets in every Home Screen size and on the Lock Screen, and a Person widget for one person you choose. Touch and hold a widget and tap Edit Widget to choose who it shows. Within seven days it counts down in days, hours and minutes.

How do I add a photo?

Open a person, tap the pencil, and tap Add a photo, choose a picture and move or zoom it inside the circle. Adjust, Replace and Remove appear once a photo is added. You can also use their initials or pick an icon. Card style lets you use the photo as the card background or as a faint watermark.

How do I send a greeting?

Open a person. Share is always there. If you saved a phone number for them (or imported them from Contacts), Message and Call buttons appear too.

Can I see everything in one place?

Tap the calendar button at the top right to see every date, month by month.

Does it work with Siri?

Yes. Try "Who is next in Recordare?" or "What is coming up in Recordare?"

Is my information private?

Yes. Your dates stay on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. We never receive them. See the Privacy Policy.

Will my dates move to a new phone or another device?

Yes, if iCloud sync is on (Settings in the app, then Sync with iCloud) and the devices use the same Apple ID. Your dates, colours, icons and small previews of photos appear on each device. Dates are also included in your iPhone backup.

iCloud sync says to sign in.

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name at the top, and sign in with your Apple ID. Then open Recordare and check Sync with iCloud is on.

How do I import birthdays from my Contacts?

Tap the person icon next to + at the top right (or open Settings, then Import birthdays from Contacts). Allow access when iOS asks, choose who to add and tap Import. People already in the app are marked Added. Your Contacts are read on your iPhone only.

I have many people. How do I find one?

Once you have eight or more people, a search bar appears at the bottom of the list.

What happens when I tap a widget?

Recordare opens on that person.

How do I delete someone?

Touch and hold their card and choose Delete, or open them and tap Delete.
