Recordare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Recordare ("the app") helps you keep track of birthdays and remembrance dates. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we never receive it.

In short

Recordare has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We do not receive your information.

What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter, such as names, dates, notes, gift ideas, phone numbers, colour choices, icon choices and any photos you pick. This information is saved on your iPhone, in the app's private storage and in a shared storage area that lets the app's own widgets show your dates. If you turn on iCloud sync it is also kept in your own iCloud account (see below). It is never sent to us.

What we collect

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code.

Contacts (optional)

If you choose Import birthdays from Contacts, iOS asks for your permission. The app then reads names, birthdays, phone numbers (used only for the Message and Call buttons) and, if you allow it, contact photos from your Contacts on your iPhone, so you can choose who to add. This happens on your device only. Nothing from your Contacts is sent to us or to anyone else, and you can turn the permission off at any time in iOS Settings. Entries you import are stored like any other entry you add.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you leave Sync with iCloud on, your entries (names, dates, notes, colour and icon choices) and small previews of your photos are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data is held by Apple under your Apple ID. We cannot see it. Full-size copies of your photos are kept in the app's own private folder in your iCloud Drive, so they look sharp on your other devices. Only you can open them, and nobody at Recordare can. Removing a photo, or the date it belongs to, removes its copy from iCloud Drive as well. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings under your Apple ID, iCloud. Turning it off stops new uploads. Copies already in iCloud Drive stay there until you remove the photo in the app or manage the app's iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device. These are local notifications created by iOS. No server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Photos

When you choose a photo for a person, the app uses Apple's system photo picker. The app only receives the single photo you select and does not get access to the rest of your library. A resized copy of the selected photo is saved on your device so it can appear on cards and widgets.

Backups

Your data may also be included in your iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup), according to your own device settings. That is controlled by Apple and by you, not by the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the information it stored on your device. If you use iCloud sync, delete the entries in the app first (or turn sync off), because deleting an entry also removes it from your iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
